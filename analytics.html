Academic Analytics Methodology & Data Integrity Rules

Weighted SGPA / CGPA: Calculated strictly from graded credits (Σ(grade_points × credits) / Σcredits). Program Cohort Benchmarks: Aggregated anonymously within your academic program excluding your own records, displayed only when at least 3 eligible peers exist. Statistical Correlations: Pearson r is calculated only when ≥ 3 matched course-semester observations with non-zero variance exist; disjoint semesters display an honest insufficient-overlap state instead of fabricated trends.
